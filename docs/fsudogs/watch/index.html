Footnotes

“Free over the air” means the game is broadcast free — not that you can necessarily receive it. A ✓ in the antenna column means the network is carrying that game over the air nationally. Whether it reaches you depends on your address, your antenna and what is between you and the transmitter, and we cannot check that from here. Most fans do not live in their team's college town, so we do not grade these cells by what one town receives.

The one signal measurement we have is Fresno 93740. All good; FOX is double the distance and off-axis. That is a modelled prediction for a single ZIP code, so real reception can be worse even there. Treat it as an example, not as your answer — every network publishes a coverage checker for your own address.

Months. We show the monthly price and how many calendar months your games span. We don't multiply them together — that would assume you cancel on time.

Kickoff times are as published by each school, in that school's own local time zone.

Prices and channel line-ups change without notice — check the provider before you buy. A carriage dispute can also remove a channel mid-season: in November 2025 a Disney/YouTube TV dispute pulled ESPN and ABC for two weeks across peak college-football Saturdays.
